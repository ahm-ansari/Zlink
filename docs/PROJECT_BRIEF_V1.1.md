# ZawajLink - Matrimonial Broker Management Portal

Brand Name: ZawajLink (زواج لينك)  
Version: 1.1  
Date: May 26, 2026  
Prepared for: Abulhassan

## Executive Summary

ZawajLink is a secure SaaS web portal for matrimonial brokers in Qatar and the GCC. It blends traditional matchmaking with modern broker CRM, privacy controls, profile verification, proposal workflow, and explainable matchmaking.

## Objectives

Business objectives:

- Reduce manual broker workload through automated profile management, search, follow-ups, and proposal tracking.
- Enable brokers to manage larger client portfolios.
- Maintain strong privacy, cultural sensitivity, and GCC-ready operating flows.
- Support adoption by brokers in Doha and GCC markets.

Technical objectives:

- Responsive Next.js frontend.
- Node.js and MongoDB backend.
- Bilingual Arabic and English UX with RTL support.
- Role-ready architecture for Broker, Admin, Support, and Limited Client View.

## Functional Modules

- Authentication and role-based access foundation
- Broker verification model
- Client biodata management
- Advanced search and match suggestions
- Proposal status tracking
- Appointment and follow-up fields
- Broker CRM pipeline
- Service fee and payment tracking
- Subscription plan model
- Admin moderation readiness

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Mobile-first usability for non-technical brokers
- Secure profile and document handling
- HTTPS-ready deployment
- MongoDB-backed scalability
- Cultural sensitivity in labels, visibility, and workflow
- Arabic/English language readiness

- MVP development: 8-12 weeks
- Testing and QA: 3 weeks
- Deployment and training: 2 weeks
